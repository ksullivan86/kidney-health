## What this page will teach

- [ ] **ACEi or ARB:** first line with albuminuria (1B). Labs 2–4 weeks after a start or change. Keep taking it unless creatinine rises > 30 %. Keep it below eGFR 30. Never combine an ACEi with an ARB.
- [ ] **Finerenone:** approved for CKD with type 1 diabetes in September 2026; potassium checks; not started if K > 5.0 mEq/L; avoid grapefruit; strong CYP3A4 inhibitors are contraindicated. FINE-ONE: UACR fell 25 % more than with placebo; hyperkalaemia 10.1 % vs 3.3 %.
- [ ] **SGLT2 inhibitors:** not approved in type 1 diabetes (DKA risk), so ask before any off-label use; if prescribed off-label, ketone checks and sick-day rules are mandatory.
- [ ] **GLP-1 receptor agonists:** not approved in type 1 diabetes.
- [ ] Pregnancy warning: ACEi and ARB harm the fetus (link to [Sex, fertility and pregnancy](../living/sex-fertility-pregnancy.md)).
- [ ] **No dose numbers** (the dose lint fails the build on them).
