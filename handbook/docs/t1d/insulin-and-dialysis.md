- [ ] **HD:** dialysate glucose content matters; lows during and after sessions; the team often adjusts basal insulin on dialysis days (JBDS); check before, during if symptomatic, and after.
- [ ] **PD:** absorbed glucose raises needs; icodextrin meter warning (use a glucose-specific meter).
- [ ] **After transplant:** steroids and tacrolimus raise glucose.
- [ ] CGM caveats.
- [ ] **No numbers for doses.**
