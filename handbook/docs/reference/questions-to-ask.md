# Questions to ask your care team

## In short

Short visits go further when you bring written questions. Pick the **three** that matter most to you
today, write the answers down, and bring a family member or friend if you can. KDIGO says education
works best when care partners are included ([KDIGO 2024][K24], practice point 5.3.2), and the US
Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality has a free question builder for any visit
([AHRQ][AHRQ-Q]). Each handbook page also ends with its own "Ask your care team" list.

## How to use this list

1. Print this page, or copy your top three questions into your phone.
2. Tick the box when a question is answered, and write the answer next to it.
3. Before you leave, say the plan back in your own words ("So I will…") to check you have it right.
4. Ask for anything important **in writing**, especially sick-day rules and medicine changes.
5. Bring your medicine list, your home blood pressure and weight readings, your CGM report and the
   app's summary ([Reports for your team](../app/reports-for-your-team.md)).

## About my kidneys and my tests

- [ ] What are my latest eGFR and UACR, and which G and A categories are they?
      ([CKD stages](../ckd/stages.md))
- [ ] Is my kidney disease from diabetes, or should we look for another cause?
- [ ] What is my 2-year and 5-year risk of kidney failure (KFRE)? ([KDIGO 2024][K24], recommendation 2.2.1)
- [ ] How often will you check my eGFR, UACR, potassium and blood count?
- [ ] Was my last change more than normal variation? What might explain it?
- [ ] Who else should be on my team: a renal dietitian, a diabetes educator, a pharmacist, a social
      worker? ([KDIGO 2024][K24], practice point 5.3.1)

## About my medicines

- [ ] Which of my medicines protect my kidneys and my heart, and what does each one do?
- [ ] Should I be on an ACE inhibitor or ARB, finerenone, or a statin?
      ([Type 1 diabetes and your kidneys](../ckd/diabetes-and-your-kidneys.md))
- [ ] Do any of my medicines need a different dose at my eGFR? ([KDIGO 2024][K24], practice point 4.2.1)
- [ ] Which over-the-counter medicines, supplements and herbal remedies should I avoid?
      ([KDIGO 2024][K24], practice point 4.1.3; [Medicines to avoid](../medicines/avoid.md))
- [ ] Which medicines do I pause when I am sick, and **exactly when do I restart them**? Will you
      write it down? ([KDIGO 2024][K24], practice point 4.3.2)
- [ ] If I might become pregnant, which of my medicines are safe? ([KDIGO 2024][K24], practice point 4.1.4)

## About food and fluid

- [ ] Do I need to limit potassium, phosphorus, sodium, protein or fluid right now? What are **my**
      numbers? ([Eating well](../eat/index.md))
- [ ] Can you refer me to a renal dietitian?
- [ ] Which foods for treating a low fit my diet best? ([Treating a low](../t1d/treating-a-low.md))
- [ ] Will you check the targets in my app and change any that are wrong for me?
      ([Targets and warnings](../app/targets-and-warnings.md))

## About type 1 diabetes

- [ ] What A1c or time-in-range goal is safe for me now? ([KDIGO 2022][K22], recommendation 2.2.1)
- [ ] Is my A1c still reliable at my stage, or should we use my CGM report?
      ([KDIGO 2022][K22], practice point 2.1.2)
- [ ] Are my lows changing as my kidneys change? Should my insulin plan be reviewed?
- [ ] When should I check ketones, and which number means I call you or go to the emergency room?
      ([Sick days](../t1d/sick-days.md))
- [ ] Is my glucagon in date, and has my family been shown how to use it?

## About blood pressure and my heart

- [ ] What blood pressure goal is right for me? Should I check it at home, and how?
- [ ] Is my cholesterol treated? Should I take a statin?
- [ ] What activity is safe for me? ([Exercise](../living/exercise.md))

## When kidney function is falling (G4–G5)

- [ ] When should I start learning about dialysis, a transplant and care without dialysis?
      ([KDIGO 2024][K24], practice points 2.2.2–2.2.3; [Choosing a treatment](../prepare/choosing-a-treatment.md))
- [ ] Can I join kidney disease education classes? Medicare covers up to 6 sessions at stage 4
      ([Medicare][MED-KDE]).
- [ ] Can I be referred for a transplant evaluation **before** I need dialysis?
      ([KDIGO 2024][K24], practice point 5.4.3; [Transplant referral](../prepare/transplant-referral.md))
- [ ] Should I protect the veins in one arm for a future fistula?
      ([Dialysis access](../prepare/dialysis-access.md))
- [ ] Is home dialysis possible for me?

## On dialysis

- [ ] What is my dry weight, and how much can I safely gain between sessions?
- [ ] Are my dialysis numbers (adequacy, potassium, phosphate, albumin) where they should be?
      ([Dialysis adequacy](../labs/dialysis-adequacy.md))
- [ ] How should my insulin and meals change on dialysis days?
      ([Insulin and dialysis](../t1d/insulin-and-dialysis.md))
- [ ] Who do I call out of hours, and what happens if I miss a session?

## After a transplant

- [ ] Which signs mean I call the transplant center right away?
- [ ] How will my anti-rejection medicines affect my glucose?
- [ ] Which foods and vaccines should I avoid now?

## Before any scan, surgery or new medicine

- [ ] Does this scan use contrast dye? Have you seen my latest eGFR? ([Scans and contrast](../medicines/scans-and-contrast.md);
      [ACR–NKF consensus][CON20])
- [ ] Should I pause any medicine before the procedure, and when do I restart it?
      ([KDIGO 2024][K24], practice point 4.3.3)
- [ ] Is this new medicine safe at my eGFR, and does it affect my potassium or glucose?

## For family and caregivers

- [ ] Can I come to the education sessions? ([KDIGO 2024][K24], practice point 5.3.2)
- [ ] What should I do if they have a severe low? Where is the glucagon?
- [ ] What are the warning signs for this stage, and who do I call? ([Get help now](../get-help-now.md))
- [ ] Where can I get support for myself? ([Caregivers](../living/caregivers.md))
