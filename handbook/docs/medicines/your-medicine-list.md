## What this page will teach

<!-- Writers: copied from docs/dev/research/08-handbook-site.md §5. Turn each item into page text using

- [ ] One up-to-date list (template), one pharmacy if possible.
- [ ] Doses checked against eGFR by the prescriber or pharmacist (the list records what you take; it never suggests a dose).
- [ ] A review at every transition (hospital, new specialist, dialysis start, transplant).
- [ ] Your written sick-day plan and restart dates.
- [ ] A printable card.
- [ ] Sources to cite: KDIGO 2024 PP 4.2.1, 4.3.1–4.3.2, 4.3.1.1; NIDDK managing CKD.
