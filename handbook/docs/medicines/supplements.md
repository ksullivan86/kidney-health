# Supplements and herbal products

--8<-- "includes/no-dosing.md"

## In short

**No supplement without your nephrologist's OK, and renal-specific vitamins only.** "Natural" does not
mean safe: several herbs damage the kidneys or interact with transplant and heart medicines, and many
powders hide potassium, phosphorus or magnesium ([NKF][NKFherb]; [AKF][AKFherb]; [KDIGO 2024][K24],
practice point 4.1.3).

## From the diet guide

- NKF lists as especially risky in kidney disease: alfalfa, oral aloe vera, aristolochia, arnica,
  astragalus, bearberry (uva ursi), cat's claw, chaparral, comfrey, creatine, goldenrod, horsetail,
  licorice root, nettle, St John's wort and yohimbe ([NKF][NKFherb]).
- AKF adds barberry, java tea, Oregon grape root, parsley root, pennyroyal and others ([AKF][AKFherb]).
- "Electrolyte", "green" and "superfood" powders, kelp, noni, dandelion and many multivitamins carry
  hidden potassium, phosphorus or magnesium ([NKF][NKFherb]).

## Ask your care team

1. Is there any supplement or vitamin I should take? Which brand?
2. Is my protein or electrolyte powder safe?
3. I take an herbal product: can you check it against my medicines?

## Get help now if…

- After a new supplement: muscle weakness, a slow or irregular heartbeat, or much less urine: call your
  team today, or 911 if severe. See [Get help now](../get-help-now.md).
