- [ ] **CT contrast:** the risk is lower than once thought. Not on dialysis with eGFR < 30: IV fluids beforehand (ACR–NKF). Do not refuse a needed scan.
- [ ] **Gadolinium MRI at G4–G5:** group II agents preferred.
- [ ] Heart catheter contrast is risk-assessed separately.
- [ ] Bowel preparations: avoid sodium phosphate preparations.
- [ ] Sources to cite: ACR–NKF 2020 and 2021; KDIGO 2024 PP 4.4.1.1–4.4.2.1; FDA sodium phosphate communication.
