# Your stage, day to day

## In short

This section has one page for each stage of chronic kidney disease (CKD) and for each kind of
treatment. Find yours in the tables below, using your latest eGFR or the treatment you have now.
Stages are **labels for risk and planning, not a countdown**: CKD leads to kidney failure only in
some people, and treatment can slow it ([NIDDK][NIDDK-whatis]). Each page says what changes at that
stage, the numbers that matter, what to do this week, and when to get help.

These pages are written for **adults**. Children and teenagers with kidney disease should be cared
for by a paediatric kidney team, and some adult advice, such as limiting protein, does not apply to
them ([KDIGO 2024][K24], practice points 3.3.1.4 and 5.1.2).

## Find your page

### Not on dialysis: use your eGFR

Your eGFR (estimated glomerular filtration rate) is on your blood test report. It is measured in
mL/min/1.73 m². The stage is set by results that last **more than 3 months**, not by one test
([KDIGO 2024][K24]).

| Your eGFR | Stage | Your page |
|---|---|---|
| 90 or more, with signs of kidney damage (such as albumin in the urine) | G1 | [Stages G1 and G2](g1-g2.md) |
| 60–89, with signs of kidney damage | G2 | [Stages G1 and G2](g1-g2.md) |
| 45–59 | G3a | [Stage G3a](g3a.md) |
| 30–44 | G3b | [Stage G3b](g3b.md) |
| 15–29 | G4 | [Stage G4](g4.md) |
| under 15 | G5 | [Stage G5 without dialysis](g5-without-dialysis.md) (also for comprehensive conservative care) |

### On treatment: use your treatment

| You are… | Your page |
|---|---|
| on hemodialysis at a dialysis centre | [In-centre hemodialysis](hemodialysis-in-centre.md) |
| on hemodialysis at home (standard, short daily or overnight) | [Home hemodialysis](home-hemodialysis.md) |
| on peritoneal dialysis (CAPD by hand, or APD with a night cycler) | [Peritoneal dialysis](peritoneal-dialysis.md) |
| being assessed for, or waiting for, a kidney or pancreas-kidney transplant | [Before a kidney transplant](before-transplant.md) and your current stage or dialysis page |
| living with a kidney or pancreas-kidney transplant | [After a kidney transplant](after-transplant.md) |
| choosing not to have dialysis | [Stage G5 without dialysis](g5-without-dialysis.md) |

Choosing between treatments is covered in [Choosing a treatment](../prepare/choosing-a-treatment.md)
([NIDDK: choosing a treatment][NIDDK-choosing]).

## Your numbers

### The second part of your stage: albumin in the urine

Your stage also has an "A" number from the urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (UACR). Risk rises with
both numbers, so "G3a A3" is higher risk than "G3b A1" ([KDIGO 2024][K24]). See
[CKD stages](../ckd/stages.md) and [UACR](../labs/uacr.md).

=== "US units"

    | Category | UACR |
    |---|---|
    | A1 (normal to mildly raised) | under 30 mg/g |
    | A2 (moderately raised) | 30–300 mg/g |
    | A3 (severely raised) | over 300 mg/g |

=== "International"

    | Category | UACR |
    |---|---|
    | A1 (normal to mildly raised) | under 3 mg/mmol |
    | A2 (moderately raised) | 3–30 mg/mmol |
    | A3 (severely raised) | over 30 mg/mmol |

### Your own risk number (KFRE)

From stage G3 on, your team can work out your personal chance of needing dialysis or a transplant
with the **Kidney Failure Risk Equation (KFRE)**. That number, not the stage alone, sets the timing of
the next steps ([KDIGO 2024][K24], recommendation 2.2.1 and practice points 2.2.1–2.2.3):

| Your KFRE result | What it is used for |
|---|---|
| 3–5 % or more over 5 years | seeing a kidney specialist (nephrologist) |
| more than 10 % over 2 years | joining a kidney care team (diet, medicines, education and social support) |
| more than 40 % over 2 years | learning about treatments, planning dialysis access, transplant referral |

KFRE **may not be valid at G1 and G2**, so it is not used there ([KDIGO 2024][K24], practice point 2.2.4).

## What to do

These apply at every stage:

- [ ] Find your latest eGFR and UACR and write them down, with the date.
- [ ] Open your stage page above and read its "What to do" list.
- [ ] Treat every low blood glucose straight away. Potassium or fluid limits never delay it
      ([ADA 2026 §6][A26-6]; [Treating a low](../t1d/treating-a-low.md)).
- [ ] Keep glucagon at home and teach someone how to use it ([ADA 2026 §6][A26-6]).
- [ ] Ask your team for a written sick-day plan ([Sick days](../t1d/sick-days.md)).
- [ ] Never eat star fruit or drink its juice, at any stage. It contains a toxin that failing
      kidneys cannot clear ([NKF][DG17]).
- [ ] Keep one up-to-date medicine list ([Your medicine list](../medicines/your-medicine-list.md)).
- [ ] Put your stage, dialysis days and targets into the app
      ([First setup](../app/first-setup.md)).

## Examples

- **"My eGFR went from 47 to 43. Am I worse?"** Results move from test to test. KDIGO says a change
  of more than 20 % on a later test is more than normal variation ([KDIGO 2024][K24], practice point
  2.1.3). 47 to 43 is a change of about 9 %. Ask your team which stage they use for you.
- **"I have been G3b for six years."** That can happen: CKD leads to kidney failure only in some
  people ([NIDDK][NIDDK-whatis]). The stage tells you what to check and plan for. Your KFRE tells you
  how fast things are likely to change.

## Ask your care team

1. Which stage do you use for me, G and A?
2. What is my KFRE, and when will you work it out again?
3. Which page of this handbook fits me best right now?
4. Which of the starting numbers above apply to me, and what are my own numbers?

## Get help now if…

!!! danger "Call 911 (or your local emergency number)"
    Chest pain, severe breathlessness or not being able to lie flat, fainting, new confusion or a
    seizure, a low the person cannot treat themselves (give glucagon, then call), or possible
    diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA): blood ketones of 3.0 mmol/L or more, or any ketones with vomiting,
    deep fast breathing or fruity breath. With kidney failure, DKA can come with lower ketone
    readings, so the signs count too ([2024 consensus][HC24]). Full list:
    [Get help now](../get-help-now.md).
