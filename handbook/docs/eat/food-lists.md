# Food lists

## In short

These lists sort common foods by how much potassium, phosphorus and sodium they have **at the
serving shown**. They are a starting point: a food on the "avoid" list can be fine for someone whose
blood potassium is normal, and a food on the "eat" list can be wrong for someone on a fluid limit.
**Star fruit is never allowed** at any stage of kidney disease ([NKF][DG17]; [de Oliveira 2015][DG39]).
Values are approximate USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy) figures ([FDC][FDC]); when a food has a
label, the label wins.

## How the foods are sorted

| | Low | Medium | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Potassium per serving (AKF) | 150 mg or less | 151–250 mg | 251 mg or more (NKF: 200 mg or more) |
| Phosphorus per serving (AKF) | 100 mg or less | 101–199 mg | 200 mg or more, or **any** "phos" ingredient |
| Sodium per serving (FDA) | 140 mg or less ("low sodium") | | 460 mg or more (20 % DV) |

([AKF potassium guide][DG46a]; [AKF phosphorus guide][DG46b]; [NKF][DG5]; [NKF][DG20]; [FDA][DG34].)
**Bold** marks a value over a "high" line.

Grapefruit and grapefruit juice change the blood level of some medicines: the labels for finerenone
(Kerendia) and tacrolimus (Prograf) say to avoid them ([Kerendia label][KER]; [Prograf label][PROGRAF]).
Ask your pharmacist about your own medicines.

!!! note "On dialysis"
    On hemodialysis the high-potassium list stays off (2,000–3,000 mg a day); on peritoneal dialysis
    potassium is often more liberal and your dietitian may allow some foods back; on either, protein
    portions rise to 4–6 oz and phosphate binders go with meals ([KDOQI 2020][Q20]; [DaVita][DG25a]).

## What to do

- [ ] Pick most fruit and vegetables from the "Eat" list, in ½-cup portions.
- [ ] Have no more than one or two "Limit" foods per meal.
- [ ] Ask your dietitian which "Avoid" foods you can have back, given your own labs.
- [ ] Never eat star fruit, in any form.
- [ ] Look up any food in the app: it shows the same numbers and a green, yellow or red rating.

## Ask your care team

1. Which "Avoid" foods are fine for me with my current labs?
2. How many "Limit" foods can I have a day?
3. Can you give me a list of my favorite foods that fit?

## Get help now if…

- For urgent symptoms (very high potassium, a low you cannot treat), see [Get help now](../get-help-now.md).
