## What this page will teach

<!-- Writers: copied from docs/dev/research/08-handbook-site.md §5. Turn each item into page text using

- [ ] **US:** Medicare ESRD eligibility (work credits), start dates (usually the first day of the fourth month of dialysis; earlier with home-dialysis training), end dates (12 months after dialysis stops or 36 months after a transplant), the 30-month coordination period with employer plans, Medicare Advantage (open to people with ESRD).
- [ ] 2026 amounts: Part B premium $202.90/month, deductible $283; Part B-ID $121.60/month. **Update `figures_as_of` every November.**
- [ ] The $35/month insulin cap on Medicare.
- [ ] Kidney disease education sessions (up to 6 at stage 4).
- [ ] Medicaid, SSDI, AKF HIPP grants.
- [ ] **Outside the US:** dialysis and transplant are usually covered by national systems; check the national kidney charity (Kidney Care UK grants as an example) and travel insurance.
- [ ] This is the only page where "ESRD" is used (Medicare's legal category).
