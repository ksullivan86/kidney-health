## What this page will teach

- [ ] **This is a referral page, not a how-to.** Pregnancy with CKD and type 1 diabetes is planned with a nephrologist, a diabetes team and maternal-fetal medicine.
- [ ] ED and low libido are common and can be treated, so ask.
- [ ] Fertility falls with stage and returns after a transplant, so use contraception.
- [ ] **Pregnancy risk rises with stage:** plan with a nephrologist and maternal-fetal medicine.
- [ ] **ACEi and ARB harm the fetus:** plan the switch *before* trying (with the prescriber).
- [ ] Mycophenolate REMS. Wait at least 1 year after a transplant.
- [ ] Type 1 preconception A1c < 6.5 % if it can be reached safely.
- [ ] Dialysis during pregnancy needs more sessions.
- [ ] Sources to cite: UK Kidney Association 2019; KDIGO 2024 PP 4.1.4; ADA 2026 §15.
