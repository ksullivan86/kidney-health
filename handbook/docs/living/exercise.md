- [ ] **≥ 150 min/week** of moderate activity, or as tolerated; avoid sitting for long periods; falls advice.
- [ ] **Type 1:** carbohydrate and bolus planning (no dose numbers); check ketones if glucose > 270 mg/dL (15 mmol/L); no exercise with blood ketones ≥ 1.5 mmol/L.
- [ ] Exercise during dialysis.
- [ ] Access-arm and PD-catheter cautions; ask your unit about swimming.
- [ ] Sources to cite: KDIGO 2024 Rec 3.2.2.1, PP 3.2.2.2–3.2.2.3; exercise consensus 2017; ADA 2026 §5.
