*[CKD]: chronic kidney disease
*[eGFR]: estimated glomerular filtration rate: how well the kidneys filter, from a blood test
*[GFR]: glomerular filtration rate: how well the kidneys filter
*[UACR]: urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio: how much albumin (protein) leaks into the urine
*[A1c]: a blood test of average glucose over the last 2–3 months (HbA1c)
*[AKI]: acute kidney injury: a sudden drop in kidney function over hours or days
*[KRT]: kidney replacement therapy: dialysis or a kidney transplant
*[HHD]: home hemodialysis
*[CDC]: US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention
*[NHS]: National Health Service (UK)
*[ISPD]: International Society for Peritoneal Dialysis
*[AHRQ]: US Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality
*[KDIGO]: Kidney Disease: Improving Global Outcomes, the international kidney guideline group
*[KDOQI]: Kidney Disease Outcomes Quality Initiative of the US National Kidney Foundation
*[ADA]: American Diabetes Association
*[NKF]: National Kidney Foundation (US)
*[AKF]: American Kidney Fund
*[NIDDK]: US National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases
*[USDA]: US Department of Agriculture
*[FDA]: US Food and Drug Administration
*[HD]: hemodialysis
*[PD]: peritoneal dialysis
*[CAPD]: continuous ambulatory peritoneal dialysis (exchanges by hand during the day)
*[APD]: automated peritoneal dialysis (a cycler machine at night)
*[CGM]: continuous glucose monitor
*[AID]: automated insulin delivery (a pump that adjusts insulin from CGM readings)
*[TIR]: time in range (on a CGM report)
*[GMI]: glucose management indicator: an A1c-like number estimated from CGM data
*[DKA]: diabetic ketoacidosis, a dangerous build-up of ketones
*[ACEi]: angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitor, a blood pressure medicine that protects the kidneys
*[ARB]: angiotensin receptor blocker, a blood pressure medicine that protects the kidneys
*[MRA]: mineralocorticoid receptor antagonist (for example finerenone)
*[SGLT2i]: sodium-glucose cotransporter-2 inhibitor
*[NSAID]: non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drug (for example ibuprofen or naproxen)
*[NSAIDs]: non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (for example ibuprofen or naproxen)
*[ESA]: erythropoiesis-stimulating agent, an injection that helps make red blood cells
*[PTH]: parathyroid hormone
*[TSAT]: transferrin saturation, an iron test
*[KFRE]: Kidney Failure Risk Equation
*[SPK]: simultaneous pancreas-kidney transplant
*[RDN]: registered dietitian nutritionist
*[CDCES]: certified diabetes care and education specialist
*[RDA]: recommended dietary allowance
*[DV]: Daily Value on a US Nutrition Facts label
*[IDWG]: interdialytic weight gain: weight gained between dialysis sessions
*[FMLA]: US Family and Medical Leave Act
*[SSDI]: US Social Security Disability Insurance
*[TSA]: US Transportation Security Administration
*[ESRD]: end-stage renal disease, the legal name Medicare uses for kidney failure treated with dialysis or a transplant
*[OTC]: over the counter (no prescription needed)
*[KCl]: potassium chloride
