!!! danger "No doses on this page"
    This handbook never gives insulin or medicine doses and never tells you to change one.
    Your prescriber sets every dose and every change, and writes down when to pause a medicine
    and when to restart it. A low blood glucose is always treated straight away:
    see [Treating a low](../t1d/treating-a-low.md) and [Get help now](../get-help-now.md).
