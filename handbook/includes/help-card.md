**Kidney disease + type 1 diabetes: get help**

**Call 911** (112 EU · 999 UK · 000 Australia): chest pain · can't breathe or lie flat ·
fainting, very slow or irregular pulse · lab potassium 6.5 or more · stroke signs (note the time) ·
a low I can't treat: glucagon, turn me on my side, call 911 · ketones 3.0 or more, or ketones with
vomiting · fistula bleeding not stopped after 10–15 min of firm pressure · fever with confusion or
shaking chills

**Call my team now or today:** potassium 6.0–6.4 · ketones 1.6–2.9 (now; emergency room if no
answer) · vomiting, diarrhea or not eating · cloudy PD fluid (keep the bag) · no fistula buzz ·
missed dialysis · fever over 100 °F after a transplant · lows that keep coming back
