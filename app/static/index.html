          Import from a spreadsheet

          A CSV file with a date column and one column per test, for example Date, Potassium (mmol/L), Creatinine (umol/L). Patient portals and spreadsheet apps can usually save one. The file is read on this device: you check every result before saving, and only the results you keep are sent.

          Up to 1 MB and 1,000 rows.

          Import from Apple Health (clinical records)

          If you linked your health system in the iPhone Health app, its lab results are in the Health export as small files. They are read on this device and nothing is uploaded: you check every result before saving, and only the results you keep are sent.

          1. In the Health app, tap your picture (top right), then Export All Health Data, and save the zip to Files.
          2. In Files, tap the zip to unzip it, then open apple_health_export and clinical-records.
          3. Back here, choose the files below, go to clinical-records, tap Select, then Select All.
          Up to 2,000 files, 1 MB each. Only lab results the app keeps are read; names and other details in the files are ignored.

          Import from a patient portal file (C-CDA .xml)

          Patient portals such as MyChart can give you your health summary as an XML file. It is read on this device and nothing is uploaded: you check every result before saving, and only the results you keep are sent.

          1. In your patient portal (for example MyChart), open your health summary or a visit summary and choose Download. If it offers a choice of formats, pick the XML one (also called C-CDA or CCD), not the PDF. The names of the menus differ between portals.
          2. Back here, choose the .xml file below. You can choose several.
          Up to 20 files, 5 MB each. Only the Results section is read, and only lab results the app keeps; names and other details in the files are ignored.

          Read a lab report photo

The AI on your own network copies the printed results; nothing is saved until you check each one against the report, tick it and press Save. The report may show your name and date of birth, so crop the photo to the results table if you can.

          The photo is sent to your local AI and is not kept.

                            Keep my data in this browser

                            For trying the app without a server.

                            This demo can save what you enter in this browser, so it is still here the next time you open it. It needs no server and no account.

                            • Your data stays only in this browser, on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
                            • Clearing site data, private browsing, or the browser deciding to free up space can delete it. Export it regularly (Settings, Your data) and keep the file somewhere safe.
                            • On a shared device, other people who use this browser can see it. The demo has no real sign-in.
                            • For real use, run your own server: it keeps your data on a computer you control, with sign-in and backups.

                            Read on this device. Nothing is uploaded until you press Import, and then only the weights and readings you keep.

                            Only weights and blood pressure readings are read. Nothing else in the file is looked at: not heart rate, steps, blood glucose or insulin.
